Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the technology, pilot process, data-center strategy and investment participation.

How does the water-cycle system work?
A water-cycle power generation system can generate electricity with only a certain continuous flow of water, without the need for the water level difference required by traditional hydroelectric power stations.
Does water flow alone guarantee production?
No. Recoverable power depends on flow, pressure, head, elevation, pipe configuration and efficiency.
Would FARAD add a turbine to a pumped loop?
Not without a net-energy analysis. Additional pumping may consume more energy than the generator produces.
Which facilities are strongest candidates?
Wastewater plants, pressure-reducing stations, gravity-fed pipelines, reclaimed-water networks and industrial water systems.
Is this a general power source for data centers?
No. The strongest opportunities involve utility or reclaimed-water infrastructure serving a campus.
What does a credible pilot measure?
Flow, pressure, head, gross and net output, pumping impacts, downtime, maintenance and system performance.
Are the other technologies validated?
They remain under evaluation and require independent documentation.
How can an organization request evaluation?
Provide location, facility type, flow, pressure, elevation, operating schedule and an engineering contact.
What investors is FARAD seeking?
Financial and strategic investors with relevant relationships, expertise and market access.
Does FARAD guarantee results or returns?
No. Performance and economics remain subject to engineering review, validation and written agreements.
