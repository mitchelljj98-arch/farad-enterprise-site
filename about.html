About FARAD Energy Systems

FARAD is focused on practical opportunities to recover energy from water flow, pressure and industrial infrastructure.

Our Mission

Connect promising energy-recovery technologies with facilities that have appropriate operating conditions while validating technical and economic claims before large-scale deployment.

Our Vision

Help infrastructure operators recover useful energy from pressure and elevation conditions that would otherwise be dissipated.
