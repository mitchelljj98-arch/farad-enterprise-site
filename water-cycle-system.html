Primary commercial focus

Water-Cycle Power Generation System

A proposed hydraulic energy-recovery system for high-flow water, wastewater, reclaimed-water and industrial infrastructure.

Recover Energy From Existing Hydraulic Conditions

The system is intended to convert existing pressure, gravity flow or elevation energy into electricity where water is already moving continuously.

  • Technically suitable high-flow infrastructure
  • Potential continuous operation
  • Safe bypass and controls
  • Net-output evaluation
