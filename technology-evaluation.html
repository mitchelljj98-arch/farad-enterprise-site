Technology Evaluation

FARAD evaluates emerging technologies according to identifiable input energy, net output, operating effects, safety and independent validation.

Identify the Energy Source

Every generator requires a measurable external input.

Measure Net Output

Include auxiliary loads, drag, pumping effects and losses.

Validate Independently

Use credible laboratory or field testing.
